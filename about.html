About Us

Serve and Empower

Serve and Empower is a nonprofit organization focused on empowering communities, supporting youth, promoting mental health awareness, and creating social impact.

Founder

Christine Chitswanda is a Political Science student at the University of Zimbabwe. She is passionate about leadership, youth empowerment, and community development.

Mission

To empower communities through education, service, and outreach programs.

Vision

To build strong, united, and empowered communities across Zimbabwe.

Goal

To inspire youth and create positive social impact through volunteering.
